Not every input is a grid of pixels or a left-to-right sequence. This short district covers architectures built for data with a different shape: networks of relationships, continuous-time processes, and models that grow a team of specialists instead of one block.

Topics on this stretch of the road:

* [[Graph Neural Networks]]: learning from data that is a network of related items.
* [[Neural ODEs]]: treating a network's depth as continuous time instead of discrete layers.
* [[Mixture of Experts]]: routing each input to a handful of specialist sub-networks instead of running the whole model.
