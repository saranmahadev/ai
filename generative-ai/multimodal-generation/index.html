Multimodal Generation

Generative AI1 min read

Every section so far generates one modality at a time. This short section covers models built to generate across modalities from one shared representation, rather than being a separate model per modality glued together.

Topics in this section:

  • Unified Multimodal Generation: one model, one shared representation, more than one kind of output.
  • Any-to-Any Generative Models: taking in any supported modality and generating any other, through a single interface.
