Biological and Historical Foundations

Neural Networks1 min read

Before there was backpropagation, there was a question: what is a neuron actually doing, and can a simple rule explain how it learns? This section starts from the real, biological cell, through the first mathematical model of it, to the first learning rule — and explains why the rest of this subject takes such a different shape from Deep Learning.

Topics in this section:

  • The Biological Neuron: how a real neuron fires, and what an artificial neuron keeps and discards from it.
  • Hebbian Learning: "cells that fire together wire together" — learning with no error signal at all.
  • The McCulloch-Pitts Neuron: the first mathematical model of a neuron, a decade before the perceptron.
  • Why This Planet Looks Different from Deep Learning: what unites the topics ahead, and why none of them train by plain backpropagation.
