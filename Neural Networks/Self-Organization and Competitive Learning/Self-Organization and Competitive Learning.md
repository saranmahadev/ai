No labels, no reconstruction target, no energy function — just units competing to be the one that responds to each input, and the winner (and its neighbours) adjusting to respond even better next time. This short section covers that family of unsupervised, competition-driven learning.

Topics in this section:

* [[Self-Organizing Maps]]: a grid of units that arranges itself to mirror the shape of the data.
* [[Competitive Learning and Vector Quantization]]: the winner-take-all rule underneath self-organizing maps, on its own.
