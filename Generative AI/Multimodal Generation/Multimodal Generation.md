Every district so far generates one modality at a time. This short district covers models built to generate across modalities from one shared representation, rather than being a separate model per modality glued together.

Topics on this stretch of the road:

* [[Unified Multimodal Generation]]: one model, one shared representation, more than one kind of output.
* [[Any-to-Any Generative Models]]: taking in any supported modality and generating any other, through a single interface.
