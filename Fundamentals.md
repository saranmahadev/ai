Welcome to the start of the road. **AI Fundamentals** answers the questions everything else builds on: what counts as AI, what an intelligent system is made of, how it perceives, decides and acts, and how we judge whether it works. There is no maths here, only ideas, small examples and simulations you can play with.

By the end you will be able to take any AI system, from a thermostat to a chatbot, and describe it as a **goal**, some **data**, a **model**, a **decision loop** and a way to **judge** it. The later planets, from [[Math]] to [[Agents]], fill in each piece.

## The road

1. [[What Is AI]]: what the word covers, how AI, machine learning and deep learning nest, and why we learn rules from data instead of writing them.
2. [[The Agent]]: the unit every AI system is built around, and the perceive → decide → act loop.
3. [[Data and Models]]: what data is, what a model is, and the difference between training and using one.
4. [[Classic AI]]: search, heuristics, logic, planning and expert systems, the methods AI used before learning took over.
5. [[Judging AI]]: how to measure a system, whether it generalises, where it fails, and how the whole picture fits together.

From here the road leads on to [[Math]], the language every model is written in, and then [[Machine Learning]].
