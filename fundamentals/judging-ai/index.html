Judging AI

AI Fundamentals1 min read

Building an AI system is only half the job. The other half is judging it: how do you know it works, whether it will keep working on new cases, and where it is likely to fail? This district gives you the habits for asking those questions before trusting a system.

Topics on this stretch of the road:

  • Measuring Performance: what to count, and why accuracy alone can mislead.
  • Generalization: doing well on examples it has never seen.
  • Where AI Goes Wrong: biased data, changing worlds and confident mistakes.
  • Anatomy of an AI System: pulling the whole planet together, and a map of what comes next.

Generalization

Generalization is a model's ability to do well on examples it has never seen. A model that merely memorises its training examples, noise and all, scores perfectly on them and poorly on anything new.
