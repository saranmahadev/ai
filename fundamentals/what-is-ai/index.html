What Is AI

AI Fundamentals1 min read

This district answers the first question: what is AI? The honest answer is that the word covers a family of ideas, and which systems count depends on how you define it. You will learn to define it usefully, place it inside machine learning and deep learning, and see why learning from examples took over from hand-written rules.

Topics on this stretch of the road:

  • What Counts as AI: several definitions, and why a thermostat is or is not AI depending on which one you choose.
  • AI, ML, DL and Generative AI: how the big terms nest inside each other.
  • Rules vs Learning: why we let machines learn from examples.
  • Narrow, General and Super AI: what today's systems can and cannot do.
  • A Short History of AI: booms, winters and the ideas that came back.

What Counts as AI

Artificial intelligence has no single agreed definition, so whether something "counts" depends on the question you ask.

AI, ML, DL and Generative AI

AI, machine learning, deep learning and generative AI are nested ideas, like Russian dolls.

Rules vs Learning

There are two ways to make a machine behave well: write the rules by hand, or show it examples and let it work the rules out. Hand-written rules are clear but brittle.

Narrow, General and Super AI

Narrow AI does one job, or a small family of jobs, very well. General AI would handle a wide range of tasks the way a person can. Superintelligence would go far beyond human ability.

A Short History of AI

AI's history is a pattern of big promises, real breakthroughs, disappointment and funding cuts, then a new approach and a new wave. The two long slumps are called AI winters.
