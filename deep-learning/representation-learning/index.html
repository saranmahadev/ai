Representation Learning

Deep Learning1 min read

A trained network's real product is not just its predictions but the representations its hidden layers learned along the way. This section looks at what those representations are, how to see them, and how to reuse them on a new problem.

Topics in this section:

  • Autoencoders: a network trained to reconstruct its own input through a narrow bottleneck.
  • Transfer Learning and Fine-Tuning: reusing a network trained on one task as the starting point for another.
  • Feature Visualization: looking at what a hidden unit actually responds to.
  • What Deep Networks Learn: why early layers learn generic features and later layers learn task-specific ones.
  • Self-Supervised Learning: manufacturing labels from the data itself, with no human annotation.
  • Contrastive Learning: learning representations by pulling related views together and pushing unrelated ones apart.
