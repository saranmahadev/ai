Trees turn learning into a flowchart of yes/no questions, and ensembles combine many trees into something far stronger. This section covers how a tree is grown and how it chooses its questions, why it must be held back, and the two great ways of combining trees: averaging independent ones in a forest, and adding corrective ones in sequence with boosting.

Topics in this section:

* [[Decision Trees]]: questions, leaves and rectangular regions.
* [[Impurity and Splits]]: Gini, entropy and information gain.
* [[Pruning and Overfitting]]: how big a tree should be.
* [[Random Forests]]: many different trees voting.
* [[Gradient Boosting]]: trees that fix each other's mistakes.
