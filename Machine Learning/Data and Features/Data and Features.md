Models see numbers, not the world, so how data is cleaned and represented decides most of what a model can learn. This district covers the practical craft: dealing with holes and dirt, putting features on comparable scales, turning categories into numbers, creating better inputs, and the strange geometry of having too many dimensions.

Topics on this stretch of the road:

* [[Missing Values and Cleaning]]: blanks, outliers and why the reason matters.
* [[Scaling and Normalisation]]: putting features on comparable footing.
* [[Encoding Categories]]: integer codes, one-hot and their hidden claims.
* [[Feature Engineering]]: making the pattern easier to see.
* [[The Curse of Dimensionality]]: why more features can hurt.
