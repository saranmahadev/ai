Not every learner fits a formula. Some remember examples and compare new ones with them; others count how often things happen and apply Bayes' rule; others lift the data into a space where a straight cut suffices. This district covers nearest neighbours, naive Bayes and its text application, the difference between modelling the classes and modelling the boundary, and kernels.

* [[Generative vs Discriminative Models]]: modelling the classes or the boundary.
* [[Kernel Methods]]: curved boundaries from straight cuts in a lifted space.
