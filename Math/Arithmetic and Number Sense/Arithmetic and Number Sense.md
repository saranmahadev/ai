Before vectors and gradients come the numbers themselves. This district makes sure the everyday machinery is solid: what kinds of numbers exist, how fractions and percentages relate, in what order to calculate, what powers and logarithms do, how to handle huge and tiny scales, how rounding behaves, and how to tell when an answer is plausible.

Topics on this stretch of the road:

* [[Numbers and Their Kinds]]: naturals, integers, rationals and reals, and why the difference matters.
* [[Fractions, Ratios and Percentages]]: three ways to say the same thing.
* [[Order of Operations and Properties]]: which step comes first, and which rearrangements are safe.
* [[Exponents and Roots]]: repeated multiplication and its undoing.
* [[Scientific Notation and Scale]]: writing very large and very small numbers.
* [[Logarithms]]: counting multiplications instead of size.
* [[Rounding, Absolute Value and Modulo]]: floors, ceilings, distance from zero and wrap-around.
* [[Estimation and Sanity Checks]]: getting the right ballpark before the exact answer.
* [[Reading Math Notation]]: the symbols and Greek letters you will meet from here on.
