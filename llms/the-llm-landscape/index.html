The LLM Landscape

LLMs1 min read

Outside the technical mechanics, LLMs exist inside a fast-moving ecosystem of model families, release decisions, licenses and governance frameworks that shape who can actually build on what. This district covers that landscape.

Topics on this stretch of the road:

  • A History of LLM Families (GPT, BERT, T5, Llama): how today's major model lineages trace back to a handful of architectural decisions.
  • Open-Weight vs. Closed Models: what "open" actually means for a model, and what it doesn't.
  • Model Cards and Responsible Release: the documentation that's supposed to accompany a released model, and why it matters.
  • Licensing and Model Governance: the legal terms that determine what you're actually allowed to do with a model.
  • Responsible Scaling Policies and Frontier Safety: how major labs frame commitments about increasingly capable models.

Two LLMs with similar benchmark scores can offer radically different things to the people using them, depending on whether the weights themselves are published or only an API sits in front of them.

Model Cards and Responsible Release

A model card is a short document that ships alongside a model, stating what it was trained on, what it's good and bad at, and where it's known to fail.
