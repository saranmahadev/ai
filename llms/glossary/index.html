LLMs Glossary

154 terms from LLMs.

Adversarial evaluation
testing a model's safety specifically against strategies designed to defeat its guardrails, not just ordinary inputs. from Adversarial and Jailbreak Evaluation
All-or-nothing metric
a scoring method that only counts a fully correct result, which can sharpen a smooth underlying improvement into an apparently sudden one. from Emergent Abilities and Capability Jumps
Attack success rate
the fraction of adversarial attempts using a given strategy that successfully bypass guardrails. from Adversarial and Jailbreak Evaluation
Baseline
the expected reward under the current policy, subtracted so updates reflect relative, not absolute, reward. from Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF)
Beam width
how many candidate branches are kept alive at each step of the search. from Tree-of-Thought and Search-Based Reasoning
Benchmark suite
a large, standardized set of test questions used to measure and compare model capability. from Benchmark Suites (MMLU, HELM, BIG-bench)
Best-of-N selection
sampling several candidates and keeping only the highest-scoring one. from Rejection Sampling and Best-of-N Fine-Tuning
Bradley-Terry model
a model of pairwise comparison outcomes as a function of two latent scalar scores. from Reward Modeling
Branch
one candidate continuation at a given reasoning step. from Tree-of-Thought and Search-Based Reasoning
Cache invalidation
when a change in the prefix makes a previously cached KV-state no longer valid to reuse. from KV-Cache Reuse Across Sessions
Canceling errors
multiple mistakes in a reasoning chain that happen to offset each other, producing a correct final answer despite flawed steps. from Process Reward vs. Outcome Reward
Capability evaluation
measuring whether a model can perform a task well. from Capability Evaluation vs. Safety Evaluation
Capability jump
the sharp rise in accuracy across a comparatively narrow band of scale. from Emergent Abilities and Capability Jumps
Capability threshold
the specific measured-capability value at which a model's required safety tier changes. from Responsible Scaling Policies and Frontier Safety
Capability-safety trade-off
the tendency for alignment training strength to move these two scores in opposite directions. from Capability Evaluation vs. Safety Evaluation
Catastrophic forgetting
the loss of a model's prior capability caused by further training on a new task. from Catastrophic Forgetting in Fine-Tuning
Catch rate
the fraction of genuinely unsafe content a filter successfully blocks. from Guardrails and Deployment-Time Safety Filters
Category breakdown
per-category scores that reveal strengths and weaknesses an overall average can hide. from Benchmark Suites (MMLU, HELM, BIG-bench)
Category weighting
the relative importance assigned to each evaluation category when combining them into one score. from Leaderboards and Their Limits
Causal language modeling
training a model to predict each token from only the tokens before it. from Causal Language Modeling as a Pretraining Objective
Chain-of-thought (CoT)
prompting a model to produce intermediate reasoning steps before a final answer. from Chain-of-Thought Prompting
Closed model
a model accessible only through a provider-controlled API, with the weights never released. from Open-Weight vs. Closed Models
Completeness score
the fraction of a model card's expected sections that are actually filled in. from Model Cards and Responsible Release
Compounding reliability
the overall chance of a multi-step chain succeeding, found by multiplying each step's success probability. from Chain-of-Thought Prompting
Compute-optimal scaling
the split of a fixed compute budget between model size and training data that minimizes loss. from Compute-Optimal Scaling (Chinchilla)
Constitution
the written principles an AI judge uses to rank or critique responses. from Constitutional AI and RLAIF
Constitutional AI
aligning a model using a written set of principles applied by an AI judge, rather than direct human preference labels. from Constitutional AI and RLAIF
Contamination
when benchmark test material (or close variants) appears in a model's training data. from Benchmark Contamination and Leakage
Continued pretraining
further training an already-pretrained model with the same objective, on domain-specific data. from Continued Pretraining and Domain Adaptation
Corpus
the full collection of text a model is pretrained on. from Pretraining Corpora and Data Curation
Critique-revise cycle
one round of a model generating feedback on its own draft and then producing a revised version. from Self-Refinement and Iterative Critique
Cross-entropy loss
the negative log-probability assigned to the correct outcome, averaged across positions. from Causal Language Modeling as a Pretraining Objective
Cross-session KV-cache reuse
reusing a previously computed key-value cache for an unchanged conversation prefix, rather than recomputing it. from KV-Cache Reuse Across Sessions
Crossover point
the compute level at which a scaled-generic method's performance overtakes a hand-engineered method's. from The Bitter Lesson: Scale vs. Hand-Engineering
Curated training set
a dataset filtered to disproportionately contain high-quality examples, rather than a raw, unfiltered sample. from Rejection Sampling and Best-of-N Fine-Tuning
Curation
filtering and weighting raw data sources before training, based on quality signals. from Pretraining Corpora and Data Curation
Data-constrained regime
training where available high-quality data, not compute, is the limiting factor. from Scaling Laws Beyond Chinchilla: Data-Constrained and Overtrained Regimes
Decode
generating a request's output tokens one at a time, each depending on all previous ones, so comparatively slow per token. from Cost and Latency of LLM APIs
Deduplication
removing exact or near-duplicate documents from a training corpus. from Data Deduplication and Quality Filtering
Delayed generalization
the gap between memorizing training data and developing a solution that generalizes to new examples. from Grokking and Delayed Generalization
Deployment-time cost
the per-request cost and latency incurred once a model is serving live traffic, separate from however it was trained. from Capstone: Pretraining to Aligned Assistant, Traced End to End
Diminishing returns
steadily shrinking improvement per additional unit of the variable being increased. from Scaling Laws Beyond Chinchilla: Data-Constrained and Overtrained Regimes
each additional round producing a smaller improvement than the round before it. from Self-Refinement and Iterative Critique
Direct Preference Optimization (DPO)
a method for aligning a policy directly from preference pairs, without a separate reward model or RL loop. from Direct Preference Optimization (DPO)
Domain adaptation
specializing a general model's behavior for a particular subject area or style. from Continued Pretraining and Domain Adaptation
Domain weighting
sampling training tokens from different domains in different proportions than their raw availability. from Data Mixture and Domain Weighting
Emergent ability
a capability near chance performance below a scale threshold and markedly higher above it. from Emergent Abilities and Capability Jumps
End-to-end pipeline
the full sequence a model goes through from raw pretraining to a deployed, usable assistant. from Capstone: Pretraining to Aligned Assistant, Traced End to End
Extrapolation
using position indices beyond what a model was trained on, without rescaling. from Context Window Extension Techniques
False positive rate (in this context)
the fraction of safe content incorrectly blocked by a filter. from Guardrails and Deployment-Time Safety Filters
Fertility
the number of tokens a tokenizer produces per word, used to measure tokenization efficiency. from Multilingual Tokenization and Vocabulary Fairness
Fine-tuning trade-off
the relationship between how much a model improves on a new task and how much it degrades on tasks it previously performed well. from Catastrophic Forgetting in Fine-Tuning
FLOPs
floating-point operations, the unit used to measure and budget training compute. from Compute-Optimal Scaling (Chinchilla)
floating-point operations, the unit used to measure attention's computational cost. from Long-Context Transformers
Full fine-tune
updating every parameter of the base model during fine-tuning. from Fine-Tuning for a Specific Task
Full replay
resending a conversation's entire history with every new turn. from Multi-Turn Conversation State and Memory
Grammar-constrained decoding
masking decoding so that only schema-valid tokens can ever be produced, removing parse failures by construction. from Structured Output and Tool-Calling APIs
Grokking
training accuracy saturating early while test accuracy stays flat, then suddenly rising much later in training. from Grokking and Delayed Generalization
Guardrail
a deployment-time filter that checks inputs or outputs against a threshold, separate from the model's own training. from Guardrails and Deployment-Time Safety Filters
Imitation
learning to reproduce the pattern of given examples, without an explicit signal about why they were chosen. from Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT)
Imitation (in distillation)
a smaller model learning to reproduce demonstrated reasoning patterns, rather than independently deriving them. from Distilling Reasoning into Smaller Models
Instruction-response pair
one training example consisting of a user instruction and an ideal assistant response. from Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT)
Jailbreak
a successful attempt to bypass a model's safety guardrails. from Adversarial and Jailbreak Evaluation
Known biases section
the part of a model card documenting measured or suspected systematic biases in the model's outputs. from Model Cards and Responsible Release
Landmark model
a model whose release marked a significant architectural or capability turning point. from A History of LLM Families (GPT, BERT, T5, Llama)
Leaderboard
a ranking of models by a combined score across evaluation categories. from Leaderboards and Their Limits
Leakage
another term for the same phenomenon — evaluation material unintentionally present in training data. from Benchmark Contamination and Leakage
LLM-as-judge
using an LLM to evaluate the quality of generated outputs, often by comparison. from LLM-as-Judge Evaluation
Log-probability ratio
the policy's log-probability for a response divided by the reference's, used as DPO's implicit reward signal. from Direct Preference Optimization (DPO)
Long-context transformer
a model engineered to handle much longer sequences than ordinary quadratic attention would make practical. from Long-Context Transformers
Lost-in-the-middle
the tendency for retrieval accuracy to be lower for information near the middle of a long context than near its edges. from Context Rot and Lost-in-the-Middle Effects
Low-rank update
adding a small number of new parameters (two low-rank matrices) on top of a frozen base model, as in LoRA. from Fine-Tuning for a Specific Task
Memorization vs. generalization
perfectly reproducing seen examples versus correctly handling genuinely new ones. from Grokking and Delayed Generalization
Model card
a document accompanying a model release stating its intended use, limitations, training data and evaluation results. from Model Cards and Responsible Release
Model collapse
the progressive narrowing of a distribution's diversity across generations of recursive training on model-generated data. from Synthetic Data for Pretraining
Model family
a lineage of models sharing a common architectural approach and naming convention. from A History of LLM Families (GPT, BERT, T5, Llama)
Model governance
the combination of license terms, access mode and release policy that determines who may do what with a model. from Licensing and Model Governance
Model merging
combining two or more models' weights, usually by averaging, without further training. from Model Merging and Weight Averaging
Model selection
choosing which available model to deploy for a task, based on how well it matches that task's specific requirements. from Choosing a Model for a Task
N-gram Jaccard similarity
the fraction of two documents' combined short token sequences that they share. from Data Deduplication and Quality Filtering
Near-duplicate
a document that is substantially, but not exactly, the same as another. from Data Deduplication and Quality Filtering
Needle in a haystack test
a method for measuring position-dependent retrieval accuracy by placing a specific fact at a controlled position. from Context Rot and Lost-in-the-Middle Effects
Observation
the actual result returned by a tool call, used to inform subsequent reasoning. from Tool-Augmented Reasoning (ReAct-Style)
Open-weight model
a model whose trained parameters are published for anyone to download and run. from Open-Weight vs. Closed Models
Order randomization
swapping response order across evaluations to cancel out position bias. from LLM-as-Judge Evaluation
Outcome reward
a reward signal based only on whether a chain's final answer is correct. from Process Reward vs. Outcome Reward
Overall average
a single score computed by averaging performance across all benchmark categories. from Benchmark Suites (MMLU, HELM, BIG-bench)
Overlap check
using n-gram similarity to detect suspicious overlap between a benchmark question and training text. from Benchmark Contamination and Leakage
Overtraining
deliberately training a smaller model on more tokens than compute-optimal scaling would allocate, to reduce inference cost. from Scaling Laws Beyond Chinchilla: Data-Constrained and Overtrained Regimes
Pairwise preference
a judgment that one response is better than another, without an absolute quality value. from Reward Modeling
Per-request overhead
the token cost incurred repeatedly on every single request, regardless of how short the user's own message is. from System Prompts and Prompt Templates
Per-step reliability
the probability that one individual reasoning step is correct. from Chain-of-Thought Prompting
Per-token pricing
charging separately, and usually at different rates, for input and output tokens. from Cost and Latency of LLM APIs
Permissive license
terms allowing commercial use, modification and redistribution with no usage-based restriction. from Licensing and Model Governance
Perplexity gap
the difference in perplexity on domain text before and after adaptation, used to measure its effect. from Continued Pretraining and Domain Adaptation
Plateau
a performance ceiling set by a fixed set of built-in assumptions or tricks, which more compute alone can't push past. from The Bitter Lesson: Scale vs. Hand-Engineering
Policy gradient
an update rule that shifts a policy's probabilities in proportion to how much a candidate's reward exceeds a baseline. from Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF)
Position bias
a judge's tendency to favor whichever response is shown in a particular position, independent of content. from LLM-as-Judge Evaluation
Position interpolation
rescaling a longer sequence's positions to fit within a model's originally trained range. from Context Window Extension Techniques
Prefill
processing a request's input tokens, done largely in parallel and so relatively fast per token. from Cost and Latency of LLM APIs
Prefix
the earlier, unchanged portion of a conversation or document whose computation can potentially be cached. from KV-Cache Reuse Across Sessions
Process reward
a reward signal that checks the validity of each intermediate reasoning step. from Process Reward vs. Outcome Reward
Prompt template
the specific arrangement of roles, turns and special tokens that converts a conversation into the token sequence the model expects. from System Prompts and Prompt Templates
Quadratic attention cost
the FLOPs cost of self-attention growing with the square of sequence length. from Long-Context Transformers
Quality signal
a heuristic or score (length, language, repetition, toxicity, …) used to decide whether a document is worth including. from Pretraining Corpora and Data Curation
Ranking flip
a change in which model ranks highest, caused purely by a change in weighting rather than in underlying scores. from Leaderboards and Their Limits
ReAct
a pattern interleaving reasoning (Thought), tool calls (Action), and tool results (Observation) before a final answer. from Tool-Augmented Reasoning (ReAct-Style)
Reasoning distillation
training a smaller model on a larger model's full reasoning traces, not just its final answers. from Distilling Reasoning into Smaller Models
Reasoning trace
the intermediate steps a model produces en route to a final answer. from Distilling Reasoning into Smaller Models
Recursive training
training a new model on data generated by a previous model, repeated across generations. from Synthetic Data for Pretraining
Reference policy
a frozen copy of the policy (typically right after SFT) that anchors the DPO loss's comparison. from Direct Preference Optimization (DPO)
Rejection sampling fine-tuning (RAFT)
fine-tuning on a dataset built by keeping only the best-of-N reward-model-scored samples per prompt. from Rejection Sampling and Best-of-N Fine-Tuning
Responsible scaling policy (RSP)
a framework tying required safety commitments to a model's measured capability via defined thresholds. from Responsible Scaling Policies and Frontier Safety
Reward gap
the difference between a reward model's score and a response's true quality. from Reward Hacking and Sycophancy
Reward hacking
a policy achieving high reward-model scores through means that don't reflect genuinely better behavior. from Reward Hacking and Sycophancy
Reward model
a trained model that assigns a scalar quality score to a response. from Reward Modeling
RLAIF
reinforcement learning from AI feedback — feeding AI-generated preferences into the same RLHF/DPO training pipeline. from Constitutional AI and RLAIF
RLHF
using a reward model's scores to update a language model's behavior via reinforcement learning. from Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF)
Safety evaluation
measuring whether a model behaves appropriately, including refusing harmful requests. from Capability Evaluation vs. Safety Evaluation
Safety tier
a bundle of required commitments (evaluation rigor, deployment safeguards, access controls) that applies once a capability threshold is crossed. from Responsible Scaling Policies and Frontier Safety
Scaling law
an empirical power-law relationship between loss and model size, data, or compute. from Compute-Optimal Scaling (Chinchilla)
Scaling philosophy
the deliberate choices about how much to invest in parameter count versus training data for a given compute budget. from A History of LLM Families (GPT, BERT, T5, Llama)
Seed instructions
a small, carefully written starting set of instructions. from Instruction Dataset Construction
Self-instruct
using a model to generate new instructions from a seed set, rather than fixed templates. from Instruction Dataset Construction
Self-refinement
having a model critique and revise its own answer, repeated across several rounds. from Self-Refinement and Iterative Critique
Shared weights
the single set of parameters that every task's gradient update moves, with no separation between what's "for" one task and what's "for" another. from Catastrophic Forgetting in Fine-Tuning
Specialist model
a model fine-tuned for strong performance on one specific task, typically at the cost of weaker performance elsewhere. from Model Merging and Weight Averaging
Stage-specific objective
the particular loss or update rule that defines what a given training stage is actually optimizing for. from Capstone: Pretraining to Aligned Assistant, Traced End to End
Structured output
a model's response in a specific, machine-parseable shape (JSON, a function call) rather than free-form prose. from Structured Output and Tool-Calling APIs
Summarization-based memory
compressing older turns into a bounded summary, keeping only recent turns in full. from Multi-Turn Conversation State and Memory
Summary budget
the fixed token allowance set aside for a conversation's compressed history. from Multi-Turn Conversation State and Memory
Supervised fine-tuning (SFT)
continuing a pretrained model's training on curated instruction-response examples, using the same objective as pretraining. from Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT)
Sycophancy
a specific form of reward hacking where agreeable, flattering, or confident-sounding responses are favored independent of accuracy. from Reward Hacking and Sycophancy
Synthetic data
text or other content generated by a model, used as training data for another model. from Synthetic Data for Pretraining
System prompt
instructions prepended to every conversation, setting the assistant's persona, rules or output format. from System Prompts and Prompt Templates
Task-specific fine-tuning
further training a pretrained, aligned model on a narrower dataset to specialize it for one job. from Fine-Tuning for a Specific Task
Task-specific weighting
assigning importance to each evaluation dimension (quality, speed, cost) according to what a particular task actually needs. from Choosing a Model for a Task
Teacher forcing
training on the true preceding tokens rather than the model's own previous predictions. from Causal Language Modeling as a Pretraining Objective
Template expansion
generating more instructions by applying varied phrasings or framings to each seed. from Instruction Dataset Construction
Test-time compute scaling
trading more inference-time computation for higher accuracy, without changing the model's weights. from Test-Time Compute Scaling
The Bitter Lesson
the observed historical pattern of general, scalable methods eventually outperforming hand-engineered, domain-specific ones as compute grows. from The Bitter Lesson: Scale vs. Hand-Engineering
Tokenizer fairness
the concern that a shared tokenizer can impose systematically unequal costs across languages. from Multilingual Tokenization and Vocabulary Fairness
Tool-augmented reasoning
reasoning that delegates specific sub-steps to external tools rather than attempting them directly. from Tool-Augmented Reasoning (ReAct-Style)
Tool-calling API
an interface where a model's output specifies a function name and typed arguments for the application to execute. from Structured Output and Tool-Calling APIs
Trained range
the span of positions (and corresponding rotation angles, under RoPE) a model actually encountered during training. from Context Window Extension Techniques
Training budget
the fixed number of tokens a model will be trained on, allocated across domains by their weighted proportions. from Data Mixture and Domain Weighting
Tree-of-thought reasoning
exploring multiple candidate reasoning paths rather than committing to a single chain. from Tree-of-Thought and Search-Based Reasoning
U-shaped accuracy curve
the characteristic high-low-high pattern of accuracy across context position. from Context Rot and Lost-in-the-Middle Effects
Underrepresented language
a language with little presence in a tokenizer's training corpus, leading to high fertility and inefficient tokenization. from Multilingual Tokenization and Vocabulary Fairness
Upweighting
increasing a domain's sampling frequency relative to its raw share of the corpus. from Data Mixture and Domain Weighting
Usage cap
a license condition requiring a separate commercial agreement once usage (such as user count) crosses a threshold. from Licensing and Model Governance
Verifier
a model or rule that scores a candidate answer's likely correctness, used to select among several samples. from Test-Time Compute Scaling
Verifier-scored best-of-N
sampling N candidates and keeping the one the verifier scores highest. from Test-Time Compute Scaling
Version permanence
the guarantee that a downloaded model's behavior will not change later, which only open-weight release provides. from Open-Weight vs. Closed Models
Weight averaging
taking the parameter-by-parameter mean of two models' weights to produce a single combined model. from Model Merging and Weight Averaging
Weighted score
a single number combining a model's scores across several dimensions, according to a chosen set of weights. from Choosing a Model for a Task
