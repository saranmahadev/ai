Before machine learning took over, AI meant **searching, reasoning and planning** with hand-built knowledge. These methods are still everywhere, in route finders, game engines, schedulers and rule engines, and they are the clearest way to see what it means for a machine to *decide* rather than just calculate.

Topics on this stretch of the road:

* [[Search]]: exploring possibilities systematically to find a path or a plan.
* [[Heuristics and A-star Search]]: using an informed guess to search far less.
* [[Logic and Knowledge Representation]]: facts, rules and inference.
* [[Planning]]: finding a sequence of actions that reaches a goal.
* [[Expert Systems and Their Limits]]: the rule-based boom, and why it ran out of road.
