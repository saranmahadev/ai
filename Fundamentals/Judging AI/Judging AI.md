Building an AI system is only half the job. The other half is **judging** it: how do you know it works, whether it will keep working on new cases, and where it is likely to fail? This section gives you the habits for asking those questions before trusting a system.

Topics in this section:

* [[Measuring Performance]]: what to count, and why accuracy alone can mislead.
* [[Generalization]]: doing well on examples it has never seen.
* [[Where AI Goes Wrong]]: biased data, changing worlds and confident mistakes.
* [[Anatomy of an AI System]]: pulling the whole subject together, and a map of what comes next.
