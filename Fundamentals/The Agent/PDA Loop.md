The **Perception → Decision → Action** loop is a simple way to understand how an intelligent AI agent interacts with its environment.

### Perception 👁️ — “What is happening?”

**Perception** is the process by which an AI system **collects information from its environment and understands it**.

The AI may receive information through:

* Cameras → images/videos
* Microphones → sounds/speech
* Sensors → temperature, distance, motion, etc.
* Text/data → information from documents or websites

**Example:**
A self-driving car's camera detects a **pedestrian crossing the road**.

> **Input:** Raw information from the environment
> **Output:** Understanding of the current situation

---

### Decision 🧠 — “What should I do?”

After understanding the environment, the AI **analyzes the situation and chooses an appropriate action**.

The decision may be based on:

* Rules
* Goals
* Previous experience
* Machine-learning models
* Predictions about what might happen next

**Example:**
The self-driving car recognizes that the pedestrian is in its path and decides:

> **“I should slow down and stop.”**

> **Input:** Information obtained through perception
> **Output:** Selected action or plan

---

### Action ⚙️ — “Do it”

**Action** is the execution of the decision. The AI interacts with the environment to achieve its goal.

Examples:

* A robot moves its arm.
* A car applies its brakes.
* A voice assistant speaks a response.
* A recommendation system displays a movie.
* A smart thermostat changes the temperature.

**Example:**
The self-driving car **applies the brakes and stops**.

> **Input:** Decision
> **Output:** Physical or digital action

---
