Positional Information Beyond Sinusoids

Transformers1 min read

The fixed sinusoidal encoding from Deep Learning was the original transformer's answer to "where is this token in the sequence?" — but it was never the only answer, and modern models mostly use something else. This section covers the alternatives and why they displaced it.

Topics in this section:

  • Learned Positional Embeddings: treating position like any other token, with a vector learned from data.
  • Relative Position and RoPE: encoding how far apart two tokens are instead of their absolute positions.
  • ALiBi and Length Extrapolation: biasing attention directly by distance, to generalize past the training sequence length.
